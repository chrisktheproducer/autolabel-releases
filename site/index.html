Questions

Which platforms does it work with?

Whatnot, plus eBay Live and TikTok Shop LIVE in beta. Pick yours from the "Selling on" menu in the app. New platforms show up through automatic updates.

What is the "big number" on the label?

You choose: the item/rack number from your listing title (#264 prints as ITEM #264), a buyer number that stays the same all show, or sale order 1, 2, 3. Switch any time.

What are pick lists?

After the show, one click prints a label for each buyer with every item number they won, like "@jenn529 · #1, 5, 10, 264 · 4 items". Great for pulling from racks.

Do I need my computer during the show?

Yes. Sell from your phone like normal and keep BidTag open on a Windows computer connected to your printer.

Does it print shipping labels?

No. BidTag prints sorting labels during the show. You still print shipping labels from your platform after, but everything is already sorted.

What printer and labels should I use?

Any thermal label printer Windows can print to: Rollo, Munbyn, Zebra, JADENS, Phomemo and more. 2×1 removable labels are the most popular.

How does the free trial work?

Download BidTag free and open it. Click Get a key, pick Solo or Pro and start your 3-day trial, then paste the key from your email. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

What's the difference between Solo and Pro?

Same features. Solo runs on 1 computer at a time, Pro on up to 3 at once, for teams with more than one packing station.

How does the partner program work?

Apply on our partner page. Approved partners get their own link and earn a recurring monthly commission for as long as the sellers they bring stay subscribed.

How do I find my key, change plans or cancel?

Click Sign in at the top of this page (or My account in the app). Enter your email and you'll get a sign-in link to see your key, invoices and subscription.
