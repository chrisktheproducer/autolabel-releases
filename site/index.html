Questions

Which platforms does it work with?

Whatnot today. More live-selling platforms are on the way, and they'll show up in your app automatically.

Do I need my computer during the show?

Yes. Sell from your phone like you always do, and keep BidTag open on a Windows computer that's connected to your printer.

Does it print shipping labels?

No. BidTag prints small sorting labels during the show. You still print shipping labels from your platform after, but everything is already sorted by buyer.

What printer and labels should I use?

Any USB thermal label printer works. 2×1 removable labels are the most popular: they peel off clean.

How does the free trial work?

You get 3 days free with full access. Your download link and license key are emailed right away. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

Can I use it on two computers?

One computer at a time per subscription. Switching computers? Click Sign out in the app and enter your key on the new one.
